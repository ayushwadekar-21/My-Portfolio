THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEAS

Meet Ayush Wadekar.

I am a student, creative thinker, and aspiring technology enthusiast who loves exploring new ideas.

My interests span coding, web and app development, artificial intelligence, storytelling, drawing, and design. I enjoy experimenting with technology and finding creative ways to bring my ideas to life.

I am especially interested in AI and robotics. My long-term dream is to become a scientist and robotics engineer, understand how technology works, and use that knowledge to build things that make a difference.

Outside technology, I enjoy creating stories, exploring new ideas, and developing my creative skills.
